## What not to claim

- Do not state that publishing on Medium/SlideShare or GitHub upload occurred merely because the documents are prepared.
- Do not label the primary working-draft arXiv survey as journal-peer-reviewed.
- Do not present the qualitative coverage matrix as a ranked model benchmark.
- Do not say our toy ablation measures actual LLM privacy. Its perfect policy result is a fixture-level test.
- Do not claim 68.8% is a universal real-world agent leak rate or add 68.8% + 27.2% as if categories are disjoint.
- Do not set the professor's approval status yourself.
