A 15+ minute research short-story presentation by Arshan Bhanage about *Agents That Know Too Much: A Data-Centric Survey of Privacy in LLM Agents* (Lahjouji & Colaco, June 2026) and complementary 2026 surveys.

The presentation explains six data surfaces, six disclosure outcomes, and nine defense families; published AgentLeak results; benchmarks and evaluation tradeoffs; a privacy-by-design architecture; and a transparent, 16-case **synthetic**, deterministic ablation illustrating why final-answer filtering does not protect internal tool and memory channels.
